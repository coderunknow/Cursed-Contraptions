# v0.1.4 scale, animation, mechanics, and polish plan

## Request and supported scenario

- **User request:** "Build v0.1.4. The objects should be bigger, have more
  animations and more mechanics. Polish significantly." Plus the bug report:
  "the mobs when stuck, it still can be pushed out but still get damage. They
  are now ugly."
- **Confirmed scope (answered up front, before code was written):**
  1. Scale: **1.4×** the v0.1.3 footprints and heights.
  2. Mechanics: the **full bundle** — soul-charge escalation, field repair,
     shove/recapture honesty, and a per-player immunity tag.
  3. Containment: **snap back immediately and gate damage** — a captive is
     never damaged while it is outside the device.
  4. Polish: **sounds + action-bar HUD + durability wear textures**.
- **Delivery:** a pull request against `main` plus a ready-to-import
  `.mcaddon` artifact, before anything else is assumed done.
- **Compatibility guardrail:** stable `@minecraft/server` 1.17.0 (no Beta
  APIs), `min_engine_version [1, 21, 60]`, and every existing identifier, pack
  UUID, dynamic-property key, entity property, tag, and recipe unchanged, so
  worlds created with v0.1.0–v0.1.3 keep their devices, captives, durability,
  and reinforcements. New entity properties and new tags are additive.

## Root causes found

1. **Damage while outside the device.** `_performDamageCycle()` damaged its
   captive on a timer with no containment check at all, and `_containVictim()`
   only corrected a runaway captive after it drifted 2.5 blocks and never
   cleared its velocity. A shoved or knockback-launched mob therefore kept
   taking damage while visibly outside the frame.
2. **No escalation and no repair loop.** Repeatedly striking a captive had no
   consequence beyond durability loss, and a damaged device could only be
   reinforced with armor — never repaired, so a nearly broken device was
   effectively scrap.
3. **No counter-play.** A player who kept getting grabbed had no way to buy
   themselves a window of safety, and a rescue looked identical to a release.
4. **Silent feedback.** Attacks, captures, releases, repairs, reinforcement, and
   escalations all played no sound, and a captured player had no on-screen idea
   what was happening or how to get out.
5. **Small, plain models.** v0.1.3 devices were 0.88–2.06 blocks tall, which
   read as props rather than contraptions, and the wear state of a damaged
   device was invisible apart from its durability number.

## Work plan

### 1. Bigger, better-looking devices (tools/texturegen)

- Rebuild all five device definitions on a common authored scale, then apply a
  single `scale_device()` pass so an art change can never desync the model, its
  collision box, or its visible bounds.
- Derive `minecraft:collision_box` and `visible_bounds` from the finished
  geometry (world units): iron maiden **1.2 × 2.6**, cursed stocks
  **2.25 × 1.59**, gravebinder cage **2.27 × 2.62**, regret rack
  **1.78 × 2.25**, black reliquary **1.79 × 2.68**. v0.1.3 heights were
  1.81 / 1.12 / 1.50 / 0.88 / 2.06, so every device is now 2.2+ blocks tall —
  a real contraption rather than a prop.
- Repaint the atlases with broader palettes and per-device detail passes, and
  split every broad wall/plate patch into aspect-specific keys so a shared
  patch is never stretched across a thin post or an edge (the v0.1.3 smear).

### 2. More animations

- Add a **durability wear animation set** (`wear_0`…`wear_3`) plus a second
  animation controller per device, driven by `cc:anim_wear_*` tags the behavior
  pack sets as durability falls: cracks and rust appear on the frame, then the
  roof/door, then the plinth.
- Add the `torturing_high` state: when the soul charge reaches 3+, the client
  switches to a hotter torture loop from the client-synced `cc:charge` property
  — no extra server round trip.
- Add a `burst` overlay for the venting surge and keep the `strain` overlay for
  struggles. Every non-`broken` controller state remains reachable from every
  other, and `broken` stays terminal, so no animation can strand the device.
- Required animation set per device is now 15 clips (`idle`, `detect`, `close`,
  `closed`, `torture`, `torture_high`, `strain`, `burst`, `open`, `released`,
  `broken`, `wear_0`…`wear_3`), enforced by the asset verifier.

### 3. More mechanics

- **Containment contract (the bug fix).** Each device tick anchors the captive:
  mob velocity is cleared, a capped impulse pulls it back to the seat, and a
  teleport correction lands if it is genuinely out of the seat window (rate
  limited). Damage, healing, and soul-charge growth are all gated behind a
  verified containment check, so a captive that is outside is *pulled back, not
  hurt*. If a captive cannot be reseated `maxFailedReseatCycles` times it is
  released with a message instead of being left in limbo. Mobs additionally
  hold Slowness VII + Blindness, refreshed once a second.
- **Soul-charge escalation.** Every strike a contained captive survives winds
  the device one step: cycles shorten by 10 % per step and damage grows by
  30 % per step. At 4/4 the meter **vents**: a soul is harvested (dropped as
  soul shards when the frame is destroyed) and one surge strike lands at 1.5×
  the charged damage. Charge decays while the device is empty.
- **Field repair.** Interact with the device while holding its configured
  material (iron for metal frames, planks for timber, bone, or obsidian) to
  restore a fixed fraction of maximum durability. A full device refuses the
  material instead of eating it.
- **Shove / recapture honesty.** Rescued captives are teleported clear and get
  their movement back; re-entering the capture radius makes them eligible
  again, and the interaction prompt always reports the real state.
- **Immunity ward.** Sneak + use any device while holding a soul shard to spend
  it for 5 minutes of immunity: devices will not even start a capture on a
  warded player. The ward is a tag plus an expiry tick, so it survives a
  relog, and it is released with the shard only when the binding succeeds.

### 4. Polish

- **Sounds:** profile-driven vanilla cues for strike, slam, latch, release,
  reinforcement, repair, soul harvest, surge, and break. No new assets, and
  playback is wrapped so a missing id can never break gameplay.
- **HUD:** a captured player gets an action-bar line (device, state, seconds
  held, charge pips, how to get out) refreshed on change and on a slow timer.
- **Wear visuals:** cracks/rust stages as described in §2.
- **Feedback hygiene:** interaction feedback is cooldown-limited per player,
  particles are emitted between the frame and the captive (not on the frame),
  and all particle counts stay inside `performance.maxParticlesPerEvent`.

### 5. Prevent the defects from returning

- `tests/mechanics.test.js` (13 tests) plus one integration test pins the new behavior: shove →
  reseat → no damage while outside; failed reseat → release instead of damage;
  charge escalation damage sequence `[14, 18, 22, 26, 46]`; soul shard drop;
  repair; immunity; rescue; HUD; double-hit drop idempotence.
- The shared test fixtures moved to `tests/support/fakes.mjs` so the lifecycle
  suite and the mechanics suite drive the same fakes (no duplicated harness).
- `tests/validate_build.py` validates the second (wear) controller file, the
  charge/souls properties, and the client-entity controller aliases.
- `tools/texturegen/build_assets.py` refuses to write a pack whose animations
  reference missing bones, whose hitbox cannot be walked around, or whose
  controller cannot reach a required state.

## Acceptance criteria

- All five devices are visibly larger (≥ 2.2 blocks on their longest axis) and
  render with wear stages, a high-charge torture loop, and a surge overlay.
- A captive that is pushed, knocked, or shoved out of its device is pulled
  back and **takes no damage until it is inside again**; if it cannot be
  reseated it is released.
- Soul charge escalates damage and speed, vents at 4/4 for a soul, and the
  harvested souls drop as soul shards when the frame breaks.
- A damaged device can be repaired with its configured material; reinforcement
  still works and still consumes exactly one item.
- A soul shard grants 5 minutes of immunity and is consumed only on success.
- `npm test` (39 tests), `npm run typecheck`, `tests/validate_build.py`, and
  `./build.sh` all pass with zero errors before packaging.
- The handoff distinguishes automated validation from in-engine verification;
  no rendering, input, or multiplayer claim is made without a client test.

## User test matrix (Bedrock 26.2)

1. Import `Cursed-Contraptions.mcaddon` into a **copy** of the test world or
   Realm and enable both packs. Cheats are only needed for `/give` and the
   admin commands.
2. Place all five devices. Each should be noticeably larger than v0.1.3 and
   should read as a distinct contraption (cabinet, stocks, cage, rack, shrine).
3. Let a zombie or cow walk into a device. Hit it with knockback or shove it
   with a piston/water: the mob should snap back inside and take **no** damage
   while outside. Break the frame and confirm the earlier "stuck outside but
   still damaged" behavior is gone.
4. Stand in a device until the charge meter is full: the animation should turn
   hotter at 3+, then vent with a surge; check the action-bar HUD pips and the
   `/scriptevent cc:devices` soul count.
5. Break the frame after a vent and confirm soul shards drop.
6. Damage a device, then repair it while holding the matching material (iron
   for the maiden/cage, planks for the stocks/rack, obsidian for the
   reliquary). Confirm the material is consumed and the wear overlay lightens.
7. Sneak + use any device while holding a soul shard (use `/scriptevent
   cc:souls` as an admin to get a stack) and confirm the device will not claim
   you for five minutes, then works again afterwards.
8. Capture a player: confirm the action-bar status, that a teammate can rescue
   them, and that they are teleported clear with movement restored.
9. If any step fails, record the Bedrock build, platform/controls, whether both
   packs were enabled, and the Content Log/script error text.
